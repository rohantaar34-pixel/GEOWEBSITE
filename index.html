Brief Profile

Planning, GIS, and systems development built for real institutional decisions.

GeoInfometrics Solutions Corp. (GEOCORP) is one of the pioneering firms in the Philippines promoting open-source technologies in Geomatics and its wide-ranging applications.

The company supports Local Government Units and private institutions in establishing efficient Geographic Information Systems and Management Information Systems for planning, governance, and decision-making.

GEOCORP combines GIS, Planning, and MIS to deliver comprehensive, data-driven, cost-effective services that strengthen institutional capacity.
